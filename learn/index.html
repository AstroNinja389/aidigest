Inside AI: a short course

One question runs through this course: when an AI gives an answer, what is going on inside it? Researchers who study this call the field interpretability. Each lesson takes about five minutes and needs no maths.

  1. What is a "neuron" in an AI?
  2. Does each neuron mean one thing?
  3. Why ideas share neurons
  4. Finding the real building blocks
  5. Why this matters, and what is still unsolved

A disclosure: these lessons were written by Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. Anthropic's researchers produced several of the studies mentioned, so the further reading includes sources from outside the company as well, and each lesson says where the evidence is thin.
