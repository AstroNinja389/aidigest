What is an AI agent?

The short version: an AI agent is an AI program that is given a goal and then works toward it over many steps, instead of just answering one question.

Chatbot versus agent

When you ask a chatbot a question, it replies and stops. An agent is more like handing a task to an assistant. Ask it to "find the cheapest flight and book it" and it has to search, compare, make choices, and act, checking its own progress as it goes.

What makes it different

An agent can use tools: search the web, run software, read and write files, or click through websites. It decides what to do next based on what happened in the previous step. That freedom is what makes agents useful, and also what makes them harder to test, because a long chain of steps has more places to go wrong.

Why people study them

Researchers want to know how well agents really do on real tasks, how often they fail, and when they can be trusted to work unsupervised. See why it matters who runs the tests.
